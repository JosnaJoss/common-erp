### A

#### Accounts Payable (Sundry Creditors)
- **Simple Meaning:** Money the company owes to vendors for goods or services purchased on credit.
- **Real-World Example:** Owing ₹94,500 to a spice farm for 500 KG of raw chilli delivered on 30-day terms.
- **Related Screen:** Procurement -> Supplier Payments (`/purchase/payments`), Stakeholders -> Suppliers.

#### Accounts Receivable (Sundry Debtors)
- **Simple Meaning:** Money owed to the company by customers who bought goods on credit.
- **Real-World Example:** A supermarket owing ₹23,100 for delivered masala packets due in 15 days.
- **Related Screen:** Commercial & Sales -> Customer Collections (`/sales/collections`), Stakeholders -> Customers.

#### Accrual Accounting
- **Simple Meaning:** The accounting principle where revenues and expenses are recorded when they occur, regardless of when cash is exchanged.
- **Real-World Example:** Recording November factory electricity expense on November 30 even though the power bill will be paid on December 10.
- **Related Screen:** Finance -> Journal Entries / Vouchers (`/accounts/vouchers/create`).

#### Accumulated Depreciation
- **Simple Meaning:** The cumulative total amount of depreciation written off on a fixed asset since it was acquired.
- **Real-World Example:** A ₹5,00,000 delivery truck with ₹1,00,000 accumulated depreciation has a net book value of ₹4,00,000.
- **Related Screen:** Finance -> Chart of Accounts -> Fixed Assets.

#### Advance Payment
- **Simple Meaning:** Upfront money paid or received prior to invoice billing or delivery of goods.
- **Real-World Example:** Paying ₹20,000 to a farmer before harvest to book a spice lot.
- **Related Screen:** Procurement -> Supplier Payments, Staff -> Advances.

#### Aging Analysis
- **Simple Meaning:** A management report grouping outstanding invoices by how many days they are past due (e.g., 0-30 days, 31-60 days, >90 days).
- **Real-World Example:** Reviewing overdue supermarket debts older than 45 days to suspend further deliveries.
- **Related Screen:** Reports -> Customer Outstanding (`/reports/sales/customer-outstanding`).

#### Assets
- **Simple Meaning:** Tangible and intangible resources of economic value owned by a business.
- **Real-World Example:** Bank balance, warehouse stock, factory machinery, trade debtors.
- **Related Screen:** Reports -> Balance Sheet (`/reports/financial/balance-sheet`).

#### Audit Log
- **Simple Meaning:** An unalterable timestamped chronological record tracking who created, edited, or deleted data in the ERP.
- **Real-World Example:** Seeing that User `admin@erp.com` changed a customer's credit limit on 2026-11-20 at 14:32.
- **Related Screen:** Administration -> Audit Logs (`/settings/audit-logs`).

### B

#### Balance Sheet
- **Simple Meaning:** A formal financial statement showing what a business owns (Assets) and what it owes (Liabilities and Equity) on a specific calendar date.
- **Real-World Example:** Year-end statement showing Total Assets of ₹12,22,700 balanced against Liabilities and Equity.
- **Related Screen:** Reports -> Balance Sheet (`/reports/financial/balance-sheet`).

#### Bank Reconciliation
- **Simple Meaning:** The process of matching the bank balance in the ERP ledger with the official bank statement passbook.
- **Real-World Example:** Identifying a ₹15,000 cheque issued to a vendor that has not yet been presented at the bank.
- **Related Screen:** Finance -> Bank Reconciliation (`/accounts/bank-reconciliation`).

#### Batch Number (Lot Number)
- **Simple Meaning:** A unique identifier assigned to a specific production run or purchase shipment.
- **Real-World Example:** Batch `BAT-20261120-01` of Chicken Masala having manufacture date 20-Nov and expiry 19-Nov-2027.
- **Related Screen:** Operations -> Production Batches, Inventory -> Current Stock.

#### Bill of Materials (BOM)
- **Simple Meaning:** The master engineering recipe or formulation specifying exact raw materials, packaging, and labor required to produce a standard finished item.
- **Real-World Example:** Recipe requiring 35 KG chilli, 40 KG coriander, and 1,000 plastic pouches to produce 1,000 masala packets.
- **Related Screen:** Operations -> Bill of Materials (BOM) (`/operations/bom`).

#### Book Value (Carrying Value)
- **Simple Meaning:** The net value of an asset recorded on the balance sheet: `Original Cost - Accumulated Depreciation`.
- **Real-World Example:** Machinery bought for ₹10,00,000 with ₹3,00,000 depreciation has a book value of ₹7,00,000.
- **Related Screen:** Finance -> Chart of Accounts.

#### Branch / Division
- **Simple Meaning:** A physical operating office, warehouse, or retail showroom belonging to a parent company.
- **Real-World Example:** "Pune Head Office" and "Mumbai Distribution Depot".
- **Related Screen:** Enterprise Setup -> Companies & Branches (`/companies`).

### C

#### Capital (Share Capital / Equity)
- **Simple Meaning:** Total financial funds and assets invested by the business owners into the company.
- **Real-World Example:** ₹5,00,000 deposited by founders into the business bank account to launch operations.
- **Related Screen:** Finance -> Capital & Equity (`/accounts/capital`).

#### Cash Flow Statement
- **Simple Meaning:** Financial report showing the actual physical inflow and outflow of cash across Operating, Investing, and Financing activities.
- **Real-World Example:** Explaining why the company earned ₹1,50,000 profit but bank cash only increased by ₹40,000.
- **Related Screen:** Reports -> Cash Flow Statement (`/reports/financial/cash-flow`).

#### Category (Item Category)
- **Simple Meaning:** A logical group used to classify inventory items (e.g., Raw Spices, Packaging Materials, Finished Goods).
- **Real-World Example:** Classifying both "Chilli Seeds" and "Coriander Seeds" under "Raw Spices".
- **Related Screen:** Master Data -> Categories (`/master/categories`).

#### Central GST (CGST)
- **Simple Meaning:** The 50% portion of Goods and Services Tax collected on intra-state sales that is paid to the Central Government of India.
- **Real-World Example:** On a 5% GST spice sale in Maharashtra, 2.5% is CGST and 2.5% is SGST.
- **Related Screen:** Commercial & Sales -> Sales Invoices.

#### Chart of Accounts (COA)
- **Simple Meaning:** The comprehensive organized list of every financial ledger account utilized by the business.
- **Real-World Example:** A structured directory holding Asset, Liability, Equity, Revenue, and Expense accounts.
- **Related Screen:** Finance -> Chart of Accounts (`/accounts/chart-of-accounts`).

#### Closing Balance
- **Simple Meaning:** The net balance of an account at the close of an accounting day, month, or financial year.
- **Real-World Example:** HDFC Bank closing balance of ₹2,45,000 on November 30.
- **Related Screen:** Accounts -> Ledgers & Balances.

#### Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
- **Simple Meaning:** The direct expenditure incurred to produce or buy the physical products that were sold during a given period.
- **Real-World Example:** Selling goods for ₹70,000 that originally cost ₹48,600 to manufacture yields COGS of ₹48,600.
- **Related Screen:** Reports -> Profit & Loss.

#### Credit Note
- **Simple Meaning:** A legal document issued to a customer that decreases their outstanding debt due to returned goods, discount, or pricing correction.
- **Real-World Example:** Issuing a ₹2,000 credit note for 20 damaged masala pouches returned by a grocery store.
- **Related Screen:** Commercial & Sales -> Sales Returns / Credit Notes (`/sales/returns`).

#### Creditors
- **Simple Meaning:** Vendors or suppliers to whom the business owes money for goods delivered on credit.
- **Related Screen:** Stakeholders -> Suppliers.

---

### D

#### Daily Operations Entry
- **Simple Meaning:** The daily manufacturing production voucher recording materials consumed, labor used, and finished products yielded.
- **Real-World Example:** Recording the grinding of 500 KG of raw spices into 995 packaged pouches.
- **Related Screen:** Operations -> Daily Operations Entry (`/operations/daily`).

#### Day Book
- **Simple Meaning:** A daily chronological register recording all financial accounting vouchers posted on a single date.
- **Real-World Example:** Reviewing all sales, purchases, payments, and receipts entered on 23-Nov-2026.
- **Related Screen:** Reports -> Day Book (`/reports/financial/day-book`).

#### Debit Note
- **Simple Meaning:** A document issued by the buyer to a vendor reducing the payable bill amount due to rejected or returned goods.
- **Real-World Example:** Sending a ₹4,500 debit note to a farmer for 25 KG of rotten coriander seeds returned.
- **Related Screen:** Procurement -> Purchase Returns / Debit Notes (`/purchase/returns`).

#### Debtors
- **Simple Meaning:** Customers who have purchased products on credit and currently owe money to the company.
- **Related Screen:** Stakeholders -> Customers.

#### Depreciation
- **Simple Meaning:** The systematic expensing of a tangible fixed asset's cost over its estimated useful working life.
- **Real-World Example:** Depreciating a ₹12,00,000 packaging machine by ₹10,000 each month.
- **Related Screen:** Finance -> Journal Entries / Vouchers.

#### Direct Expenses
- **Simple Meaning:** Costs directly tied to manufacturing or procuring goods (raw materials, factory worker wages, factory electricity).
- **Related Screen:** Reports -> Profit & Loss (COGS section).

#### Double-Entry Bookkeeping
- **Simple Meaning:** The accounting system where every transaction requires equal and opposite debit and credit entries, keeping books in constant balance.
- **Related Screen:** Finance -> Double-Entry Accounts.

---

### E

#### Employee Code
- **Simple Meaning:** A unique organizational identifier assigned to an employee badge.
- **Real-World Example:** `EMP-0104` for a machine operator.
- **Related Screen:** Staff & HR -> Staff Directory (`/staff`).

#### Equity
- **Simple Meaning:** The net financial residual interest of the owners in the business assets after deducting all external liabilities.
- **Formula:** $\text{Equity} = \text{Assets} - \text{Liabilities}$.
- **Related Screen:** Reports -> Balance Sheet.

---

### F

#### FIFO (First-In, First-Out)
- **Simple Meaning:** The inventory valuation method assuming that the oldest stock batches purchased are the first ones consumed or sold.
- **Real-World Example:** Selling 100 KG of spice from Batch 1 (bought at ₹170) before consuming Batch 2 (bought at ₹185).
- **Related Screen:** Inventory -> Current Stock, Sales -> Sales Invoices.

#### Financial Year (Fiscal Year)
- **Simple Meaning:** The 12-month accounting cycle used for statutory tax calculation (in India: 01-April to 31-March).
- **Real-World Example:** FY 2026-27 (01-Apr-2026 to 31-Mar-2027).
- **Related Screen:** Finance -> Financial Years (`/accounts/financial-years`).

#### Fixed Assets
- **Simple Meaning:** Long-term tangible equipment, vehicles, land, or machinery owned for business operations rather than resale.
- **Real-World Example:** Factory pulverizer mill worth ₹3,50,000.
- **Related Screen:** Finance -> Chart of Accounts.

---

### G

#### General Ledger (GL)
- **Simple Meaning:** The master financial ledger containing all accounts and transactions of the business.
- **Related Screen:** Accounts -> Ledgers & Balances (`/accounts/ledgers`).

#### Godown (Warehouse)
- **Simple Meaning:** A physical building, shed, or storage location where inventory is received and stocked.
- **Real-World Example:** "Central Finished Goods Godown Shed 4".
- **Related Screen:** Master Data -> Godowns / Warehouses (`/master/godowns`).

#### Goods Receipt Note (GRN)
- **Simple Meaning:** A warehouse receiving document confirming that goods delivered by a vendor have arrived and passed physical inspection.
- **Real-World Example:** Gate pass logging receipt of 50 bags of cumin seeds.
- **Related Screen:** Procurement -> Goods Receipts (`/purchase/grns`).

#### Gross Profit
- **Simple Meaning:** Profit earned from sales after deducting direct manufacturing and procurement costs (COGS), before overheads.
- **Formula:** $\text{Gross Profit} = \text{Sales Revenue} - \text{COGS}$.
- **Related Screen:** Reports -> Profit & Loss.

#### GSTIN
- **Simple Meaning:** The 15-digit Goods and Services Tax Identification Number issued by the Government of India.
- **Real-World Example:** `27AAACR1234F1Z5`.
- **Related Screen:** Enterprise Setup -> Companies & Branches, Stakeholders -> Suppliers.

---

### H

#### Harmonized System of Nomenclature (HSN)
- **Simple Meaning:** An international standardized coding system used to classify commercial goods for taxation.
- **Real-World Example:** HSN `0910` for ginger, saffron, turmeric, and mixed spices.
- **Related Screen:** Master Data -> Items & Price Levels.

---

### I

#### Indirect Expenses
- **Simple Meaning:** General operating, administrative, and selling costs not directly tied to physical manufacturing (office rent, audit fees, marketing).
- **Related Screen:** Reports -> Profit & Loss.

#### Input Tax Credit (ITC)
- **Simple Meaning:** Credit for GST tax already paid on business purchases that reduces tax liability payable on sales.
- **Real-World Example:** Using ₹5,000 GST paid on raw spices to offset ₹8,000 GST collected on finished sales, paying only ₹3,000 net.
- **Related Screen:** Reports -> Tax / GST Summary.

#### Integrated GST (IGST)
- **Simple Meaning:** GST tax levied on inter-state commercial transactions (e.g., selling from Maharashtra to Karnataka).
- **Related Screen:** Commercial & Sales -> Sales Invoices.

#### Inventory
- **Simple Meaning:** Physical stocks of raw materials, work-in-progress, packaging supplies, and finished merchandise held by the company.
- **Related Screen:** Inventory & Godown -> Current Stock (`/inventory/current-stock`).

---

### J

#### Journal Entry
- **Simple Meaning:** A formal double-entry accounting transaction recording equal debits and credits with an audit explanation.
- **Related Screen:** Finance -> Journal Entries / Vouchers (`/accounts/vouchers`).

---

### L

#### Ledger Account
- **Simple Meaning:** A specific financial record tracking transactions and the balance for a single person, vendor, asset, or expense.
- **Related Screen:** Accounts -> Ledgers & Balances.

#### Liabilities
- **Simple Meaning:** Legally binding financial debts and obligations owed by the business to external entities.
- **Related Screen:** Reports -> Balance Sheet.

---

### M

#### Master Data
- **Simple Meaning:** Non-transactional core business entities that must exist before transactions can occur (Items, Customers, Suppliers, Godowns).
- **Related Screen:** Master Data -> Master Registry.

#### Module Activation
- **Simple Meaning:** System feature switches that enable or disable functional ERP areas per company vertical.
- **Related Screen:** Administration -> Module Activation (`/settings/modules`).

---

### N

#### Net Profit
- **Simple Meaning:** The final bottom-line earnings of the business after deducting all direct and indirect expenses, taxes, and depreciation from total revenue.
- **Related Screen:** Reports -> Profit & Loss.

---

### O

#### Opening Stock
- **Simple Meaning:** The physical quantity and financial value of inventory on hand at the start of an accounting period.
- **Related Screen:** Inventory -> Bulk Opening Stock (`/inventory/opening-stock`).

#### Outstanding
- **Simple Meaning:** The unpaid, due balance remaining on a sales or purchase invoice.
- **Related Screen:** Reports -> Customer Outstanding, Reports -> Supplier Outstanding.

---

### P

#### Petty Cash
- **Simple Meaning:** A small amount of physical cash kept on hand for minor daily office disbursements (tea, local postage, office stationery).
- **Related Screen:** Finance -> Petty Cash (`/accounts/petty-cash`).

#### Production Yield
- **Simple Meaning:** The actual quantity of good, sellable finished product produced during a manufacturing run.
- **Related Screen:** Operations -> Daily Operations Entry.

#### Purchase Order (PO)
- **Simple Meaning:** A formal commercial document sent to a supplier authorizing delivery of goods at specified rates and terms.
- **Related Screen:** Procurement -> Purchase Orders (`/purchase/orders`).

---

### R

#### Reorder Level
- **Simple Meaning:** The minimum safety inventory quantity threshold that triggers a purchase reorder.
- **Related Screen:** Master Data -> Items, Inventory -> Low Stock Alerts.

#### Revenue
- **Simple Meaning:** Gross income received from the sale of goods or provision of commercial services.
- **Related Screen:** Reports -> Profit & Loss.

---

### S

#### Sales Invoice
- **Simple Meaning:** The legal tax document issued to a customer demanding payment for sold merchandise.
- **Related Screen:** Commercial & Sales -> Sales Invoices (`/sales/invoices`).

#### State GST (SGST)
- **Simple Meaning:** The 50% portion of Goods and Services Tax on intra-state sales collected for the State Government.
- **Related Screen:** Commercial & Sales -> Sales Invoices.

#### Stock Adjustment
- **Simple Meaning:** An administrative voucher modifying system stock counts to reconcile physical inventory audit counts.
- **Related Screen:** Inventory -> Stock Adjustments (`/inventory/adjustments`).

#### Stock Transfer
- **Simple Meaning:** Moving goods from one company warehouse to another without changing ownership or value.
- **Related Screen:** Inventory -> Godown Transfers (`/inventory/transfers`).

---

### T

#### Tenant
- **Simple Meaning:** A distinct legal company hosted within the multi-company ERP system with completely isolated financial books.
- **Related Screen:** Enterprise Setup -> Companies & Branches.

#### Trial Balance
- **Simple Meaning:** An internal audit report listing every ledger balance to confirm that total debits equal total credits.
- **Related Screen:** Reports -> Trial Balance (`/reports/financial/trial-balance`).

---

### U

#### Unit of Measure (UOM)
- **Simple Meaning:** The standard physical unit used to measure inventory items (KG, Gram, Liter, Box, Piece).
- **Related Screen:** Master Data -> Units of Measure (`/master/units`).

---

### V

#### Voucher
- **Simple Meaning:** A numbered internal accounting document recording the details, debits, credits, and authorization for a financial transaction.
- **Related Screen:** Finance -> Journal Entries / Vouchers.

---

### W

#### Work in Progress (WIP)
- **Simple Meaning:** Materials that have been issued into the manufacturing process but are not yet completed finished goods.
- **Related Screen:** Operations -> Daily Operations Entry.
